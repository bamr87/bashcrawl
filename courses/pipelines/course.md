The Unix idea fits in one sentence: **small tools, each doing one thing well, joined by pipes.** In this course you'll interrogate a guild's activity log and ledger, the same way you'd interrogate a web server's access log at work. Your lab holds `guild.log`, `ledger.csv` and `names.txt`.
