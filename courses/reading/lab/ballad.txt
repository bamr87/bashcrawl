There once was a shell in the deep,
Whose prompt never fell fast asleep.
It would cat and would grep,
Never missing a step,
And its pipes ran a hundred lines steep.

The novice would type and would stare,
At commands that went who-knows-where.
But with man at their side,
And a head held with pride,
They read every last line with care.

So read before running, my friend,
From the head of a file to its end.
